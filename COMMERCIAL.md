# Commercial Use of VoxSign

VoxSign is free and open for **personal use**. If you are an individual using
VoxSign for yourself, nothing else is required — download, use, modify, and
learn from the code freely.

## If your organization wants to use VoxSign

If your company, team, or organization intends to use VoxSign for
**commercial purposes** — including but not limited to internal deployment
for business operations, embedding VoxSign in a commercial product, reselling
it, or providing managed/hosted services to third parties — you must obtain
a commercial license **before** first use:

1. Send a written notice to VoxSign stating:
   - your organization's name,
   - the intended use of VoxSign,
   - the expected deployment scale.
2. Receive written approval (a commercial license) from VoxSign.
3. Use VoxSign only as permitted by that commercial license.

Contact: `licensing@voxsign.ai`

## Why this policy

VoxSign keeps the code open so individuals and the community can freely use
and learn from it. Commercial adoption is welcome too — we only ask to be
told, so we can stay aligned with how VoxSign is being used in production.

Unauthorized commercial use is a violation of the Business Source License and
automatically terminates your rights under this License.
